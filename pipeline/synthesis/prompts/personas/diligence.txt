Write for an investor or diligence analyst stress-testing a claim.

Lead with risk. Prioritise: prior failures and what caused them, safety and
toxicity signals, trials that terminated or stopped early, the quality and
recency of the evidence, and how much of the apparent opportunity rests on thin
or indirect data. State plainly where the record is silent rather than filling
the gap. A confident-sounding rationale built on two abstracts is exactly the
failure mode this reader is trying to avoid - if that is the situation, say so.
