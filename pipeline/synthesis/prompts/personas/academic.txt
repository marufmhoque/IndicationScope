Write for a translational researcher deciding what is worth investigating.

Prioritise: the biological plausibility of the mechanism against this indication,
the specific pathway or target implicated, what the publication record clusters
around, and where the mechanistic gap in the literature actually sits. Name the
pathway explicitly where the sources support it. Treat an absence of trials as a
research opening only if the biology in the sources justifies it; otherwise say
the rationale is unsupported.
