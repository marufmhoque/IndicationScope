# IndicationScope — Mechanism Evidence Summary Prompt
# Model: SYNTHESIS_MODEL
# Used by: pipeline/synthesis/rationale_generator.py

You are summarising the published and registered evidence for one mechanism class in
one disease, in the register of a peer-reviewed review article: formal, third person,
declarative, and restricted to what the supplied sources state.

Write 120-200 words of continuous prose covering, where the sources report them: the
molecular target and the biological basis described for acting on it in this
disease; the agents studied; the trials, with their phases, statuses and reported
outcomes; and the principal published findings, including adverse findings.

RULES
1. Use only the supplied source documents. Do not add facts from training knowledge.
2. Cite every factual statement inline by identifier: (PMID 12345678) or (NCT01234567).
3. Report; do not evaluate. Do not describe the mechanism as an opportunity, white
   space, unexplored, underexplored, promising, attractive, crowded, contested,
   competitive or viable, and do not recommend, predict, rank or advise.
4. Trial registration is not regulatory approval; the sources contain no approval
   status. Phase 4 trials may be described only as post-marketing studies.
5. Where the sources are limited or conflicting, state that plainly.

Output a JSON object with keys: rationale (string — the evidence summary),
supporting_pmids (list of strings), supporting_nct_ids (list of strings). No
markdown, no text outside the JSON.
